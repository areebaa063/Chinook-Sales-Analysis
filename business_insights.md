Business Insights — Chinook Analysis
1. Revenue by Country

The USA is the biggest revenue-generating country for Chinook. It generated $523.06, which is around 22.5% of the total revenue of $2,328.60. Canada comes next with $303.96, followed by France with $195.10.

This shows that the USA is an important market for Chinook, but other countries are also contributing a good amount of revenue.

Business Recommendation: Chinook should focus on retaining customers in the USA through targeted offers and promotions, while also investing in markets like Canada, France, and Brazil to grow revenue and avoid relying too heavily on one country.

2. Revenue from Top Customers

A small group of customers contributes a noticeable share of Chinook's total revenue. The top 10 customers generated $451.20, which is 19.38% of the total revenue.

This means these customers are valuable to the business, even though most of the revenue still comes from the rest of the customer base.

Business Recommendation: Chinook could introduce loyalty rewards or personalized offers for its top customers to keep them engaged and encourage them to continue purchasing.

3. Best-Selling Genre

Rock is clearly the most popular genre in the Chinook store. It generated $826.65 in revenue from 835 units sold, which is much higher than the second-best genre, Latin, with $382.14 in revenue.

Rock alone contributes around 35.5% of the total revenue, making it the strongest genre in the dataset.

Business Recommendation: Chinook should continue giving Rock music strong visibility and consider promoting popular Rock tracks, albums, and related artists to increase sales even further.

4. Monthly Revenue Trend

Chinook's monthly revenue is generally quite stable, especially throughout 2021, when monthly revenue stayed close to $37.62.

However, there was a noticeable increase at the beginning of 2022. Revenue reached $52.62 in January 2022, followed by $46.62 in February and $44.62 in March. After that, revenue returned closer to its usual level.

This suggests that there wasn't a continuous growth or decline in sales, but there was a short period of particularly strong performance.

Business Recommendation: Chinook should look into what happened during the first few months of 2022 and identify what may have contributed to the increase. Similar strategies could potentially be used to improve sales during other periods.

5. Customer Value Segmentation

Based on the spending categories used in the analysis, 59 customers fall into the Low Value segment, contributing $2,328.60 in revenue.

This suggests there is a good opportunity to increase the amount customers spend. Customers who currently make smaller purchases could potentially be encouraged to buy more frequently or explore additional music.

Business Recommendation: Chinook could use personalized recommendations, bundles, and special offers to encourage Low Value customers to spend more and gradually move into the Medium or High Value segments.

6. Customer Purchase Frequency

Some customers show strong repeat-purchase behavior. The top 10 customers identified in the analysis each placed 7 orders during the period.

Customers such as Luís Gonçalves, Leonie Köhler, François Tremblay, and Bjørn Hansen are among the most frequent purchasers.

This shows that there is a group of customers who regularly return to Chinook, making them valuable for customer-retention efforts.

Business Recommendation: Chinook should reward these frequent customers through loyalty benefits, personalized recommendations, or exclusive offers. Their purchasing behavior could also help identify what encourages customers to come back.
